What are the areas StereoBuyers services?

We’ll buy gear anywhere, but if you’re in the NYC Tri-State Area, Palm Beach Florida, or Denver Colorado we’ll pick it up personally and pay you on the spot.

Out of our pickup range? We can still find a way for you to get your audio gear to us safely, with either expert packing advice for shipping, or via one of our regular couriers.

How does pickup work?

We come to you. We disconnect, pack up and pick up your equipment, and pay you on the spot, so there is no shipping, no waiting and nothing to lift. Free pickup covers New York, New Jersey and Connecticut, select locations in Colorado including Denver, Boulder and the Front Range, and Palm Beach Florida.

Not sure whether you are in range? Contact us or request a quote and we will tell you how we can get your gear. You can also learn how selling to StereoBuyers works.
