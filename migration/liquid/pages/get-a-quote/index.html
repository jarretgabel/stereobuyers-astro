How do I get a quote?

Fill out the form below with details about the equipment you would like to sell. We will get back to you with an offer, and if we agree on terms we pick up your equipment and pay you on the spot.

What happens after I submit the form?

We review the details you send and reply with an offer. If we agree on terms, we schedule a convenient pickup time and pay you on the spot.

What equipment does StereoBuyers buy?

We buy used, vintage and high-end home audio equipment, including amplifiers, preamps, turntables, speakers, tuners and digital players from brands such as McIntosh, Marantz, Audio Research and Luxman. The more detail you share about the brand, model and condition of each piece, the faster we can get back to you.
