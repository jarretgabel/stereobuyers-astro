Mark Levinson’s Beginnings

According to the official Mark Levinson website, Mark Levinson created the sound mixer for the famous Woodstock music festival, which helped propel his reputation. In 1972 he created “Mark Levinson Audio Systems Ltd.” in Woodbridge Connecticut, and began manufacturing high-end amplifiers. The LNP-2 Preamplifier propelled the brand to audiophile stardom, and products since have carried the flag. The original company, Mark Levinson Audio Systems Ltd., ceased operating in 1984 and its equipment and trademark rights were acquired by Madrigal Audio Laboratories.
