Luxman is a Japanese high-end audio maker known for refined, well-built amplifiers and preamplifiers, along with turntables and digital players. Its vintage solid-state and tube amplifiers from the 1970s and 1980s are sought after by collectors, and its current models remain popular with audiophiles.

Brief History of Luxman

Luxman traces its roots to Osaka, Japan, in 1925. The company built its reputation on audio electronics, and the Luxman name became widely recognized among hi-fi enthusiasts in the decades that followed. Today the brand is part of Alpine Electronics and continues to design its equipment in Japan.
