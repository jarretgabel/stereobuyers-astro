  CD-ROM STYLE 5 DISC MUSICBANKTM CHANGER. A precision mechanism originally manufactured for use as a computer CD-ROM, the MCD205 changer features an internal stocker that holds up to five CDs. It’s as easy to operate as the player in your car, yet retains the ease and capacity of a changer.

  OPTICAL AND COAXIAL DIGITAL OUTPUTS. Optical and coaxial digital outputs are provided for use with an external D/A converter. 

  NON VOLATILE PROGRAM PLAY MEMORY. A playback program, once entered, will not be lost if the power should fail.
