  • StereoBuyers is located in Brooklyn, New York, and we will pickup and pay on the spot for new or pre-owned Hi-Fi equipment, Hi-Fi accessories, and records in the greater New York City area.

    Have a large collection to sell? We can help. No collection of equipment is too large.

    If you're outside our pickup area, we can arrange for shipment. We also have offices in Boulder, Colorado, and Palm Beach, Florida.

    Please note - we do not buy car audio, pro / DJ gear, or most "mass market" audio equipment (e.g. a "home theater in a box").

    To see which brands we buy, and those we tend to avoid, please see Brands We Buy.

    We respond as quickly as possible, but please allow up to two business days.
